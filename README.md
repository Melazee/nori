# Nori

A prototype penguin partner that lives on your phone. Its mind is a free AI that runs in Chrome on the phone itself (WebGPU), so nothing you say leaves the phone.

- Open the site in Chrome on Android, then **Wake Nori's mind** (one-time download, use Wi-Fi).
- Add it to your home screen from Chrome's menu to use it like an app.
- Art: placeholder pixel penguin in `frames/`. Replace the PNGs (same names) and update `frames/frames.json` to swap the art.
